Last updated: 1 January 2025 · These documents govern your use of FurriHearts.

FurriHearts is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia.

1. Who We Are

FurriHearts is a pet adoption platform operated in Malaysia. We connect animal rescuers with prospective adopters, providing tools to list, discover, and facilitate responsible pet adoptions. For the purposes of the PDPA 2010, FurriHearts acts as the data user in respect of personal data collected through our platform.

2. Data We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account registration — name, email address, phone number, and password
  • Profile information — location, profile photo, bio (rescuers), and lifestyle preferences (adopters)
  • Adoption applications — questionnaire responses, home details, and supporting information
  • Rescuer listings — pet photos, descriptions, health information, and adoption fees
  • Communications — messages exchanged between adopters and rescuers
  • Identity verification — government-issued ID (optional, for verified badge)

Information collected automatically

  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Pages visited, time spent, and actions taken on our platform

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Facilitate pet adoption — connecting adopters with rescuers
  • Power FurriMatch — our matching algorithm uses lifestyle preferences to suggest suitable pets
  • Send transactional emails — application updates, approvals, and account notifications
  • Improve our platform through analytics
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with Malaysian law and legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only when:

  • Adoption approved — contact details are shared between adopter and rescuer to facilitate handover
  • Service providers — trusted third parties for hosting, email, and analytics, bound by data processing agreements
  • Legal obligations — if required by Malaysian law or court order
  • Business transfers — if FurriHearts is acquired, with prior notice to users

5. Data Retention

  • Active accounts — data retained for the lifetime of your account
  • Deleted accounts — deleted or anonymised within 90 days, except where law requires longer retention
  • Adoption records — retained up to 3 years for safety and dispute resolution

6. Your Rights

Under the PDPA 2010, you have the right to access, correct, withdraw consent for, or delete your personal data. To exercise your rights, visit our Contact page or email hello@furrihearts.my. We respond within 21 days.

7. Cookies

We use essential, analytics, and preference cookies. You can control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect platform functionality.

8. Children's Privacy

FurriHearts is not intended for persons under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us at hello@furrihearts.my immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date and notify registered users by email for significant changes. Continued use after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

By creating an account or using FurriHearts, you agree to these Terms of Service. If you do not agree, you may not use our platform.

1. About FurriHearts

FurriHearts is an online platform that connects animal rescuers with prospective adopters in Malaysia. We are a marketplace — we do not own, rescue, foster, or take responsibility for any animal listed on our platform. Each adoption is a direct arrangement between the rescuer and the adopter.

2. Eligibility

To use FurriHearts you must be at least 18 years of age, a resident of Malaysia or have a valid Malaysian address, provide accurate information when registering, and not have been previously suspended from our platform.

3. User Accounts

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials and for all activities under your account. Notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorised access. FurriHearts may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these Terms.

4. Adopter Responsibilities

As an adopter you agree to:

  • Provide honest, accurate information in applications and questionnaires
  • Adopt only with genuine commitment to a permanent, loving home
  • Comply with any requirements set by the rescuer, including home visits
  • Not re-home, sell, or transfer an adopted pet without notifying the original rescuer
  • Ensure your home is safe and suitable before the pet arrives
  • Provide appropriate veterinary care for your adopted pet

5. Rescuer Responsibilities

As a rescuer you agree to:

  • Provide accurate, truthful information about every animal listed, including health status and temperament
  • Ensure all animals are dewormed and flea-treated before listing
  • Disclose any known health conditions, behavioural issues, or special needs
  • Respond to applicants in a timely and respectful manner
  • Only list animals in your care that you have authority to rehome
  • Charge adoption fees solely for cost recovery, not profit
  • Remove listings promptly once an animal is adopted

6. Listings & Content

Rescuers are solely responsible for listing content. FurriHearts may remove listings that violate these Terms. By posting content, you grant us a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to display and promote it on our platform. Content must not be false, offensive, or infringe third-party rights.

7. Adoption Process

FurriHearts facilitates the connection but is not party to any adoption agreement. The adoption decision rests solely with the rescuer. We are not responsible for disputes arising from the adoption process or post-adoption animal behaviour. We encourage all parties to use an adoption contract.

8. Fees & Payments

FurriHearts is currently free for both adopters and rescuers. We reserve the right to introduce fees with 30 days' notice. Adoption fees are set by rescuers and paid directly between parties — FurriHearts does not process or guarantee these payments.

9. Prohibited Conduct

You must not use FurriHearts to sell or commercially trade animals, impersonate others, harass users, post spam, list animals not in your care, or engage in any activity violating Malaysian law including the Animals Act 1953 or Wildlife Conservation Act 2010.

10. Disclaimers & Liability

FurriHearts is provided "as is". To the fullest extent permitted by Malaysian law, we are not liable for harm to animals adopted through our platform, personal injury or property damage from adoptions, disputes between users, or any indirect or consequential losses.

11. Termination

You may delete your account anytime through account settings. FurriHearts may suspend or terminate accounts for breach of these Terms. Upon termination, your right to use the platform ceases immediately.

12. Governing Law

These Terms are governed by the laws of Malaysia. Disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of Malaysian courts. We encourage users to contact us first to resolve disputes informally.
