1. About FurriHearts

FurriHearts is an online platform that connects animal rescuers with prospective adopters in Malaysia. We provide tools for listing rescued animals, submitting adoption applications, and facilitating responsible pet rehoming.

FurriHearts is a marketplace platform. We do not own, rescue, foster, or take responsibility for any animal listed on our platform. The relationship for each adoption is directly between the rescuer and the adopter.

4. Adopter Responsibilities

As an adopter on FurriHearts, you agree to:

  • Provide honest and accurate information in your adoption applications and questionnaires
  • Adopt only if you are genuinely committed to providing a permanent, loving home
  • Comply with any requirements set by the rescuer, including home visits or post-adoption check-ins
  • Not re-home, sell, or transfer an adopted pet without first notifying the original rescuer
  • Ensure your home is safe and suitable before the pet arrives
  • Provide appropriate veterinary care for your adopted pet

5. Rescuer Responsibilities

As a rescuer on FurriHearts, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate, truthful information about every animal you list, including health status, age, temperament, and known issues
  • Ensure all animals are dewormed and flea-treated before listing
  • Disclose any known health conditions, behavioural issues, or special needs
  • Respond to applicants in a timely and respectful manner
  • Not list animals that are not in your care or that you do not have authority to rehome
  • Not charge adoption fees that are excessive or intended for profit rather than cost recovery
  • Conduct appropriate screening of prospective adopters
  • Remove listings promptly once an animal has been adopted

We encourage all parties to use an adoption contract to document the terms of each rehoming.
