Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025 · Effective date: 1 January 2025

FurriHearts is committed to protecting your personal data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA) of Malaysia. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Contents

1. Who We Are 2. Data We Collect 3. How We Use Your Data 4. Data Sharing 5. Data Retention 6. Your Rights 7. Cookies 8. Children's Privacy 9. Changes to This Policy

1. Who We Are

FurriHearts is a pet adoption platform operated in Malaysia. We connect animal rescuers with prospective adopters, providing tools to list, discover, and facilitate responsible pet adoptions.

For the purposes of the PDPA 2010, FurriHearts acts as the data user in respect of personal data collected through our platform.

2. Data We Collect

Information you provide

  • Account registration — name, email address, phone number, and password
  • Profile information — location, profile photo, bio (for rescuers), and lifestyle preferences (for adopters)
  • Adoption applications — questionnaire responses, home details, and supporting information you submit when applying to adopt
  • Rescuer listings — pet photos, descriptions, health information, and adoption fees
  • Communications — messages exchanged between adopters and rescuers through our platform
  • Identity verification — MyKad or other government-issued identification (optional, for verified badge)

Information collected automatically

  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Pages visited, time spent, and actions taken on our platform
  • Referral source (how you found us)

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Facilitate pet adoption — connecting adopters with rescuers
  • Display your profile and listings to other users where appropriate
  • Process adoption applications and notify relevant parties
  • Power FurriMatch — our pet-matching algorithm uses your lifestyle preferences to suggest suitable pets
  • Send transactional emails — application updates, approvals, and account notifications
  • Improve our platform through analytics
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Comply with Malaysian law and legal obligations

We do not use your data for automated decision-making that produces legal or significant effects on you without human review.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in the following circumstances:

  • Between adopters and rescuers — when an adoption application is approved, we share the relevant contact details of both parties to facilitate the handover
  • Service providers — we use trusted third-party services for hosting, email delivery, and analytics, all bound by data processing agreements
  • Legal obligations — we may disclose data if required by Malaysian law, court order, or a government authority
  • Business transfers — if FurriHearts is acquired or merged, your data may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you beforehand

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as required to provide our services. Specifically:

  • Active accounts — data is retained for the lifetime of your account
  • Deleted accounts — personal data is deleted or anonymised within 90 days of account deletion, except where retention is required by law
  • Adoption records — a record of completed adoptions may be retained for up to 3 years for safety and dispute resolution purposes

6. Your Rights

Under the PDPA 2010, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw consent for data processing where consent is the legal basis
  • Limit processing — request that we limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Delete your account — request deletion of your account and associated data

To exercise your rights, please visit our Contact page or email hello@furrihearts.my. We will respond within 21 days.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember your preferences, and understand how you use our platform. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some features of FurriHearts.

  • Essential cookies — required for the platform to function (login sessions, security)
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how users interact with FurriHearts
  • Preference cookies — remember your settings and preferences

8. Children's Privacy

FurriHearts is not intended for use by persons under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us immediately at hello@furrihearts.my.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and notify registered users by email if the changes are significant. Continued use of FurriHearts after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
