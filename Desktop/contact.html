Frequently asked questions

How do I apply to adopt a pet? ▾
Browse available pets, click on one you like, and hit "Apply to Adopt". You'll fill in a short questionnaire which the rescuer will review. Most rescuers respond within 3–5 days.
Is FurriHearts free to use? ▾
Yes — FurriHearts is completely free for both adopters and rescuers. Rescuers may charge an adoption fee to cover vet and care costs, which is paid directly to them. FurriHearts does not take a cut.
How do I become a verified rescuer? ▾
After creating a rescuer account, you can apply for verification by submitting your identity documents through your profile settings. Our team reviews applications within 5–7 working days. Verified rescuers get a badge that builds trust with adopters.
What happens if I suspect a listing is fake? ▾
You can report any listing directly from the pet's profile page using the "Report" button. You can also email report@furrihearts.my. We review all reports and act swiftly on confirmed violations.
Can I list dogs on FurriHearts? ▾
Yes! FurriHearts supports listings for cats, dogs, and other rescued animals. Our FurriMatch quiz and filters also support both cats and dogs.
How do I delete my account? ▾
You can delete your account from your Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account. Your personal data will be removed within 90 days in line with our Privacy Policy. If you need help, email hello@furrihearts.my.
